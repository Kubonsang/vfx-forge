# VF-021 Concept Prompt Set V2

## Shared constraints

- Use case: `stylized-concept`.
- Asset: production previsualization board for a modern top-down indie action RPG.
- Camera: one consistent high top-down three-quarter gameplay camera, 16:9 context.
- Composition: caster at lower-left, enemy target at upper-right, barrier centered between
  them. The barrier surface normal follows the caster-to-target line and faces the
  enemy. Show this through perspective and overlap, not an arrow label.
- The shield is an upright interception plane, not a disc lying on the floor and not a
  heraldic face aimed at the sky.
- Board layout: four clean panels — grayscale silhouette, full-color gameplay concept,
  the same effect over light/medium/dark grounds, and an exploded layer breakdown.
- Material proposal: deep translucent emerald energy body with broad dual-scale flow,
  controlled thin boundary charge, warm aged-gold structural frame, minimal contact
  wedge. The surface, edge, metal, and contact must be visibly separate jobs.
- GNF_ reference transfer: layered Mesh body, internal noise, boundary response,
  independent accent timing, sparse support particles. Do not copy any source Asset,
  texture, shield design, rune, or magic circle.
- Four braces or ornaments must be large, load-bearing, and physically connected to the
  frame. No detached floating glyphs.
- Avoid: upward-facing full shield face, radial UI medallion, ground sigil, mirrored
  split lobes, concentric rings, repeated runes, dense filigree, uniform emission,
  excessive sparks, text paragraphs, logos, and watermark.
- The caster and enemy must remain readable at gameplay scale.

## Candidate E — Cavalier Wall

Create a single convex enemy-facing mantlet with a broad target-side crown and a thick
caster-side root. Its outline is one continuous slightly asymmetric arch, with four
short gold shoulder braces grown into the frame. The barrier should feel heavy,
protective, and knightly; it must not resemble a round shield displayed front-on.

## Candidate F — Winged Bulwark

Create a wide shallow-V enemy-facing barrier whose two sides angle toward one central
leading ridge without becoming separate lobes. Four broad wing-like buttresses are
fused into the side frame. Keep 72% of the visual mass in the continuous emerald
barrier. Use negative space only at the outer shoulders, never through the middle.

## Candidate G — Lanceguard Prow

Create an asymmetric enemy-facing protective prow: one side advances slightly farther
toward the target, creating a clear direction and a modern authored silhouette. Use one
continuous curved energy plate, a thick diagonal leading rim, and four unequal connected
gold braces. It should read as a deployed interception device, not as a weapon or crest.

## Candidate H — Citadel Arc

Create a segment of a tall cylindrical fortress wall deployed across the caster-target
line. The target-facing leading arc is broad and clean; the caster side has a compact
root. Four large crown-like braces reinforce the upper and side frame but never exceed
the primary barrier mass. Emphasize believable thickness and elegant sweeping curvature.
