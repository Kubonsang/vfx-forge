# Candidate E model-sheet prompt V2

## Rejection incorporated

V1 improved modeling depth but changed Candidate E into an upright arch-like gate. V2
therefore changes only silhouette fidelity. V1 remains rejection evidence and is not
overwritten.

## Authoritative shape

- Candidate E is a slanted asymmetric wedge/circular-sector barrier, not a doorway.
- Front ratio is landscape-biased, approximately 1.35 times wider than tall.
- The upper-left cap is the highest point.
- A long convex crown descends toward the enemy-side right clamp.
- The lower return is shallow, concave, and descends from the raised caster-side root
  toward the heavier lower-right footing.
- There is no horizontal floor rail, paired vertical columns, centered arch opening, or
  bilateral symmetry.
- The wall remains vertical and faces the enemy instead of facing the sky.
