## Shared prompt

Create a clean 2×2 professional preproduction board for an original massive holy
aegis used by a fantasy knight in a polished contemporary top-down action RPG.

- Top-left: pure grayscale silhouette on neutral gray.
- Top-right: full-color strict-top-down gameplay concept with a small simple caster
  behind the shield and a small target beyond it.
- Bottom-left: identical gameplay framing on light, medium, and dark ground.
- Bottom-right: exploded layer breakdown for ground contact, recessed energy plate,
  structural rim/braces, and crest highlight.
- Use only `SILHOUETTE`, `COLOR`, `THREE GROUNDS`, and `LAYERS` as panel headings.
- Fixed strict orthographic top-down gameplay camera and approximately 360 px effect
  footprint within a 1920×1080 gameplay context.
- Deep nonuniform emerald energy, warm aged gold structure, restrained pale mint-white
  seam accents, broad bevels, soft contact occlusion.
- One dominant shield plate, exactly four physically connected unequal braces, one
  embedded original solar-knight crest, clean negative space, and an attractive
  grayscale silhouette.

Avoid concentric ring stacks, radial mandalas, flat yellow additive shapes, small
repeated runes, filigree, detached floating glyphs, symmetrical UI medallions,
particles that hide geometry, uniform emission, bubble domes, sci-fi hex shields,
copied game or artist motifs, extra text, and watermarks.
