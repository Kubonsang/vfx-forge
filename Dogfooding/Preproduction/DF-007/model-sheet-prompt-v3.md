# Symmetric giant shield model-sheet prompt V3

## User direction

V2 was rejected because its left-high/right-low silhouette was undesirable and still
read as a barrier. V3 intentionally supersedes Candidate E's asymmetric outline while
retaining its enemy-facing deployment and locked gameplay camera.

## Shape contract

- Perfect bilateral symmetry about the front-view vertical centerline.
- One broad heater/tower-shield plate with a centered shallow crown, equal shoulders,
  outward upper side curves, tapered lower sides, and one centered lower keel point.
- One convex defensive plate with a broad integrated center ridge.
- One thick continuous perimeter rim with authored width transitions.
- Four connected rim ornaments as mirrored pairs: two upper shoulder guards and two
  lower flank guards.
- No ground feet, horizontal base, opening, columns, fence, doorway, detached blocks,
  spikes, small runes, or filigree.
- The plate reads first, then the rim, then the four guards.

## Depth and gameplay

- Target envelope: approximately 5.2 m wide by 5.8 m high.
- Center plate depth: 0.48 m; edge depth: 0.20 m; rim thickness: 0.28 m.
- The plate is convex toward the enemy.
- It deploys in front of the caster and leans only 18 degrees toward the caster for
  top-down readability; it never faces the sky.

## Output

Four equal clay panels: front orthographic, top orthographic, right-side orthographic,
and locked high top-down gameplay perspective. No Shader, energy, particles, characters,
environment, labels, or text.
